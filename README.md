# Project Overview:

- A full end-to-end data analysis project analzying customer churn in a telecommunications company.
- **Goal**: To identify customers at risk, uncover key churn driver, and also provide business recommendations to improve customer retention.
- **Dataset**: 7043 customers, 21 features
- **Tools**: Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

# Key Findings:

- Overall churn rate: 26.5% customers churned
- Highest risk group: New customers on high chargers with 66.5% churn
- Contract impact: Month-to-month with 42.7% vs Two tear with 2.8% churn
- Payment impact: Electronic check with 45.3% churn vs approximately 15% for auto-pay
- Tenure insight: Churned customers left after average of 18 months vs 37.6 for loyal customers
- Strongest churn driver: Fiber optic internet with a correlation of 0.308
- Strongest retention factor: Long tenure with a correlation of -0.352
